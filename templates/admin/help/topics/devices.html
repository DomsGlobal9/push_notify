A device is one install of one app on one phone or tablet. The same phone with two of your apps is two devices. Devices are created by the apps themselves, never by hand.

How a phone gets here

  1. The person installs the app and signs in.
  2. The app asks for notification permission (Android 13+ and iOS ask once).
  3. The app registers with the gateway using the product's pk_ key. It does this on every launch, which keeps the push token fresh.
  4. If your backend sends the person's push identity at login, the device is linked to that user; otherwise it is anonymous and only gets product-wide pushes.

Common questions

My phone is not in the list.
Sign in to the app (it registers after login), allow notifications, and make sure the build has push switched on. Then reload this page.
A device shows Dead token.
The app was uninstalled or its data cleared. Nothing to do: it is skipped from now on. If the person opens the app again it registers as Active.
Why are there inactive devices with the same user?
Old installs and reinstalls. They are kept so you can see history; only Active ones receive pushes.
Sandbox vs Production?
iPhones running a debug build from Xcode use Apple's sandbox; TestFlight and App Store builds use production. The app reports this itself.
How do I remove a person's devices (data deletion)?
Your backend calls DELETE /v1/users/<user id>, or select the devices here and delete them.
