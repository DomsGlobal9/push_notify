What the Push Gateway does

The Push Gateway is one notification server for all of your apps. Your apps register their phones here, your backends ask it to send, and it delivers to Android through Google (FCM) and to iPhones through Apple (APNs). It retries what fails and removes phones that uninstalled.

From zero to a push on a phone

  1. Add credentials. A Firebase service account for Android, an Apple .p8 key for iOS. You need each only once, for all apps. How →
  2. Create a product. One per app family, for example "SC Boutique". The gateway shows three keys once: copy them somewhere safe. How →
  3. Add its apps. The Android package (com.sc.boutique) and the iOS bundle id, each pointing at a credential. How →
  4. Give the keys to developers. The pk_ key goes in the app, the sk_ key and identity secret on the backend only. Which goes where →
  5. Connect the app. Developers add the Flutter package; phones appear under Devices after login. How →
  6. Send a test. Send test notification → pick the product → Send. The phone buzzes within seconds.
