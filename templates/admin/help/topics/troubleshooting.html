Start from the notification: open it under Notifications and read the Failures tab. Each row's reason matches one line below.

"Sent" but the phone shows nothing

  1. App open on screen? Android shows no banner while the app is in front; the app receives it instead. Test with the app in the background.
  2. Notifications allowed? Phone Settings → Apps → the app → Notifications on, and the channel (e.g. "Orders and updates") on.
  3. Battery saver killing the app? vivo / Xiaomi / Oppo / Samsung: allow background activity and autostart for the app.
  4. Force stopped? After "Force stop" Android delivers nothing until the app is opened again. Swiping it away is fine.
  5. Do Not Disturb or focus mode on the phone.

The phone is not under Devices

  • The person has not signed in since the push-enabled version was installed: it registers after login.
  • Notification permission denied: Android 13+ and iOS need it.
  • The build has push switched off, or its package name is not registered under Apps (watch for .dev / .staging flavors).
  • The phone has no Google Play services (some Huawei phones); those cannot receive FCM.

Everything is slow or nothing goes out

  • Queue card not Healthy? Open Queue.
  • Free Render plan asleep? The uptime monitor must ping /healthz every 5 minutes. A sleeping server wakes in about a minute and then catches up; nothing is lost.
  • Scheduled? A notification with a future send_at stays Queued until then.

Backend calls fail

401Wrong or rotated key: check PUSH_GATEWAY_SECRET_KEY.
403A pk_ key was used to send, or a user link had a wrong token (identity secret mismatch).
400Read details in the answer: it names the field.
429Over 600 sends per minute for one product: batch users into one send.
