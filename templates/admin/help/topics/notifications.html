A notification is one send request: a message plus who should get it. A broadcast to 50,000 phones is still one row here; per-phone results are the counters.

The list

ColumnMeaning
MessageThe title (or body when there is no title). Click to open it.
ProductWhich app family sent it.
Status Queued waiting (or scheduled for later) · Processing sending now · Completed every phone has an outcome · Cancelled stopped before finishing

FailedRed when any phone failed or had a dead token. Open the notification to see why.

Use Filters (top right) for status and product, and the search box for a title.

Inside a notification

  • Target: who it was for: user_ids, topics, installation_ids or all, optionally narrowed by platform or app.
  • Message: title, body, data (for example route, the screen to open on tap), image, sound, priority, how long it stays valid (ttl_seconds), collapse_key.
  • Counts: targeted = sent + failed + invalid token + skipped. Skipped means the phone was unregistered between targeting and sending (normal in small numbers).
  • Failures tab: one row per failed phone with the reason from Google/Apple. Look each up in Troubleshooting.

Send a test notification

  1. Click Send test notification (top right of the list, or on the Overview).
  2. Product: which app family.
  3. User id: leave empty to send to every phone of the product, or enter a user id to reach only that person's phones (works once the backend links users).
  4. Title and Body: what people see.
  5. Open on tap (optional): an in-app path like /orders or /notifications.
  6. Click Send. You land on the new notification; it completes within seconds.
warning
An empty user id reaches everyone on that product. Use a clear test title.

Good to know

  • Notifications cannot be created or edited here except through the test form; real sends come from your backend.
  • Retries are automatic: temporary errors are retried up to 5 times with growing delays.
  • A send sent twice with the same Idempotency-Key only goes out once.
  • Finished notifications are kept 90 days; failure details 14 days.
  • Android does not show a banner while the app is open on screen; the app receives the message instead.
