The home page answers one question: are pushes going out and reaching people? Numbers cover all products and the last 24 hours unless a card says otherwise.

The five cards

CardWhat it meansWorry when
Active devicesPhones that can receive right now, split Android / iOS. Dead or unregistered phones are not counted.It drops suddenly: an app may have stopped registering.
Pushes sentPushes Google/Apple accepted in the last 24 h, and how many sends (notifications) produced them.Zero while your backend should be sending.
DeliveredSent ÷ (sent + failed + dead tokens). "Dead tokens removed" counts phones that uninstalled; they are cleaned up automatically.Below about 90%: open Troubleshooting.
OpenedTaps ÷ pushes sent. Only counted when the app reports the tap. It is a product metric, not an error signal.
QueueWork waiting to be done now. Healthy nothing older than 30 s · Falling behind oldest waiting over 30 s · Needs attention over 5 min, or a job failed for good.Anything but Healthy for more than a few minutes. Queue guide →

Last 7 days

One bar per day: sent and failed or dead tokens. Hover a bar for the exact numbers. A red slice that keeps growing means something is wrong with a credential or an app.

Products

Each product with its number of apps, active devices and when it last sent. "never" right after setup is normal. Click a name to open the product, its keys and apps.

Recent notifications

The last 8 sends across all products, with status, a progress bar and counts. Click a message to see its details and the failures tab. Sent shows sent / targeted.

refresh
