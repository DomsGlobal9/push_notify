A product is one app family: its Android and iOS apps, its devices, its notifications and its keys. "SC Boutique" is a product; a future delivery app would be another. Products never see each other's devices or sends.

Create a product

  1. Products → Add, type a name, keep Active on, Save.
  2. A yellow box shows three values, once. Copy all three now into your password manager. They are never shown again.
  3. Add its apps in the Apps tab on the product page, or under Apps.

The three keys

KeyLooks likeGoes inCan
Secret keysk_…Your backend only (an environment variable) Send to every phone of the product, list and delete users' devices
Publishable keypk_…The app (it is public by design) Only register or remove its own phone and report taps
Identity secretrandom textYour backend only Sign a token at login proving a phone belongs to a user
warning
Never put the sk_ key or identity secret in an app: anyone could extract them and message all your users.

Buttons on a product page

  • Rotate keys: creates a new sk_ and pk_ and switches the old ones off at once. Use it if a key leaked. Update the backend and ship an app update with the new pk_ first, or those stop working.
  • Show identity secret: shows it again (it is needed by every backend that links users).
  • Active switch: turning a product off makes all its keys stop working immediately; devices and history are kept.
  • API keys tab: every key ever issued, its first characters and when it was revoked.
  • History: who changed what in this dashboard.

The same actions exist on the product list: tick products → choose an action → Go.
